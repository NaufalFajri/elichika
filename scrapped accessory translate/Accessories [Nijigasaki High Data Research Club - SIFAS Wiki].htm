Accessories

Unless otherwise specified, Skills only apply to the Cards in the Strategy equipped with the Accessory. If the target is different or it's a Skill that affects the Formation as a whole (such as Healing, Shields, SP Fill or Damage Reduction), remember that the Effect Strength is effectively tripled.

DLP Accessories

DLPDLP: Short for "Dream Live Parade". A monthly singleplayer event where you try to clear as many Lives as possible with limited Card usage.➔ Learn More Accessories are special because the ranges of their effect strengths depend on the current Limit Break. Effect strength can be increased by both increasing the Skill Level and Limit Breaking the Accessory (if it's at SL2 or higher).
